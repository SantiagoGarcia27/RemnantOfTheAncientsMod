----------------------------------------
Remnant of the ancients is a mod
created for complement Terraria vanilla,
although it can use in addition to other mods to 
improve the experience.
-----------------------------------------
						
Remnant of the ancients currently adds:
				
Items:
- 3 bosses
- 30 enemies
- 1 town NPC
- 39 accessories
- 172 weapons 
- 10 Armor sets
- 4 Vanity armor sets
- 93 potions
- 54 Ammos
- 26 blocks
- 4 pets
- 14 new materials
- 1 new difficulty
- New mechanic "Style" stat
- +88 new recipes for vanilla items
- +19 tweaks and improvements to vanilla items 
- +19 new reforges
- 2 Language translation (English and Spanish) (no completed)
- Cross-mod support for the Calamity Mod,Infernum Mod,Fargos soul,Fargowitas,Thorium, BossChecklist,AlchemistNPC and Census
- 2 weapons (Calamity Mod)
- 11 accessories (Fargos Soul)
